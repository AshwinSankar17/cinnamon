The roll and its friends

The roll's face is your project's state. Its swirl is the project's history: thicker where the agent was busy, sugar crystals where it found something, a glowing tip at now. Tap it and it unrolls into the full timeline.

Needs you Something is waiting on you. Eyebrows up, eyes on the card at your thumb.
Working Jobs are running and nothing needs you. Steam rises while they run.
Proud A new finding has landed since you last looked. A sugar crystal appears on the swirl.
Asleep Brain or the tunnel is unreachable. Everything stops moving and values read “as of N min ago”.
Companions One per world. On or off from the menu. Tap one for a status line built only from verified numbers.
Crumb · sparrow, Bakery Hops along the counter. Flies to the ticket when an order is up.
Hazel · squirrel, Forest Holds one acorn per finding and tells you the best one.
Nutmeg · cat, Rustic Sleeps by the fire. Ears go up and one eye opens when you are needed.
Anise · eight-armed alien, Space One arm lights for each running job, up to eight.
Saffron · fish, Tide Swims laps, then heads for whatever needs you.
